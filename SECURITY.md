# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability or Sensitive Finding

If you discover a valid seed phrase, private key, or sensitive vulnerability related to a puzzle documented in this repository, do not open a public GitHub issue.

Contact the repository owner through a secure, encrypted channel, such as Signal or a pre-agreed secure email, to coordinate responsible disclosure and any legitimate prize claim.

Do not publish credentials, private keys, seed phrases, or wallet-draining instructions in issues, pull requests, commits, or chat transcripts.

## Ethical Guidelines

This lab operates within legal and ethical boundaries. Analysis is limited to publicly available blockchain data and open-source intelligence (OSINT). Unauthorized access, hacking, exploitation, and malicious activity are not conducted or tolerated.
